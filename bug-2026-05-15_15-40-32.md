# Travel Planner Bug & Enhancement Log

Based on the interactive testing of the Travel Planner application, here are the issues that need to be addressed. The primary focus is on fixing state management and synchronization between the UI components.

## 🔴 High Priority (Functional Bugs)

- `[ ]` **Event Persistence Bug:** New events added via the "Add Event" modal do not reliably persist or appear in the sidebar itinerary list after submission.
- `[ ]` **Day Switching Desync:** Switching between day tabs (e.g., from Day 1 to Day 2) sometimes causes the sidebar to display the incorrect itinerary list or requires multiple clicks to refresh.
- `[ ]` **Map Marker Desynchronization:** Map pins do not update when the active day changes. For example, viewing Day 2 still displays the map pins from Day 1.
- `[ ]` **Remove Event Inconsistency:** Clicking the "Remove" button visually deletes an event, but navigating away and back to the day can cause the deleted event to reappear, indicating the global state isn't updating correctly.

## 🟡 Medium Priority (UX & Polish)

- `[ ]` **Map Tile Loading (Gray Areas):** There are intermittent gray areas on the map when switching tabs or resizing. **Fix:** Ensure `map.invalidateSize()` is called when the map container's visibility or dimensions change.
- `[ ]` **Sidebar-to-Map Interaction:** Clicking an event on the map displays a popup, but it does not highlight or auto-scroll to the corresponding item in the sidebar timeline.
- `[ ]` **Missing Map Polylines:** The UI shows "Taxi" and "Transit" travel times between sidebar items, but there are no actual route lines (polylines) drawn on the map connecting the pins.

## 🟢 Low Priority (Features)

- `[ ]` **Editable Trip Title:** The main trip title ("Tokyo Adventure") and date range are static and cannot be edited by the user.

---
> [!NOTE]
> The overall premium aesthetic (glassmorphism, clean typography, marker icons) is working perfectly. The immediate goal is to fix the `zustand` state store and ensure `activeDay` is correctly respected by both the `Sidebar` and the `MapComponent`.
